How to Play & Rules

How to play

  • Tap one of your pieces and the squares it can move to are highlighted. Tap a highlighted square to move there.
  • Pieces you capture become your "pieces in hand". Tap a piece in your hand area, then tap an empty square on the board to drop it there.
  • When a move enters, leaves, or moves within the promotion zone (the opponent's three ranks), you can choose to promote the piece. A promoted piece gains the stronger movement shown on its reverse side.
  • "Undo" takes back your last move together with the computer's reply. "Resign" accepts defeat and ends the game.

How the pieces move

  • King (王将 / 玉将): one square in any direction.
  • Rook (飛車): any number of squares horizontally or vertically. Promotes to Dragon King (竜王), which also moves one square diagonally.
  • Bishop (角行): any number of squares diagonally. Promotes to Dragon Horse (竜馬), which also moves one square horizontally or vertically.
  • Gold General (金将): one square forward, sideways, diagonally forward, or straight back — everything except diagonally back.
  • Silver General (銀将): one square forward or diagonally — not sideways or straight back.
  • Knight (桂馬): jumps two squares forward and one square sideways; it is the only piece that can jump over other pieces.
  • Lance (香車): any number of squares straight forward.
  • Pawn (歩兵): one square forward.
  • When a Pawn, Lance, Knight, or Silver promotes, it moves exactly like a Gold General (と金, 成香, 成桂, 成銀).

Illegal moves & winning

  • You cannot make a move that leaves your own king in check. If your king is in check, you must get out of check.
  • Nifu is illegal: you may not drop a pawn on a file where you already have an unpromoted pawn. A pawn or lance cannot be dropped on the farthest rank, and a knight cannot be dropped on the farthest two ranks, because it would have no move.
  • Uchifuzume is also illegal: you may not drop a pawn to deliver checkmate.
  • You win by checkmating the opponent's king. If the same position occurs four times (sennichite), the game is a draw.
