Tableau — build up in gojūon order

46 cards · 10 rows · 7 columns. Face-down cards turn over when uncovered. Unlimited Undo, unlimited stock recycling, and Auto Collect are always available. Every deal can be finished.

How to Play

Goal: move all 46 hiragana to the 10 finish piles at the top — one pile for each row.

  • Finish piles: build each row in reverse order, o → e → u → i → a, so the a-kana ends on top. The A pile takes お → え → う → い → あ. The KA pile takes こ → け → く → き → か. The YA pile takes よ → ゆ → や, and the WA pile takes ん → を → わ.
  • Tableau (7 columns): place a card on the card that comes just before it in the same row. い goes on あ, う goes on い, き goes on か. A row never continues into the next one: nothing goes on お, こ, そ … (the last card of each row). You can move a whole face-up stack together.
  • Empty column: any card (or stack) can start a new column.
  • Stock: click the stock to draw one card to the waste. When the stock is empty, click it again to recycle the waste.
  • Shortcuts: double-click (or double-tap) a card to try its finish pile. Hint shows a move. Auto Collect moves every card it can to the finish piles (one Undo takes it all back). Undo takes back your last move.
  • Face-down cards turn over when the card above them is moved away.
  • Every game can be finished. Each new deal is checked before you play it. If you get stuck, the game tells you and offers to go back to the last position you can still win from.
  • The clock stops while you are away from this tab.
  • Card Colors: switch between colored cards (one light color per row) and plain white cards.
