    How to Play

    • One prefecture piece at a time appears in the box over the Sea of Japan. Drag it onto the map and drop it in the right place. Tap a piece to see its details.
    • All prefecture pieces are prepared with north at the top. They are never rotated.
    • If the shape in the pop-up has a blue line, that part of the prefecture faces the sea. The pop-up also tells you which sea it is.
    • This is the easy version: each prefecture's outline is coloured by its region, and each piece has the colour of its region — match the colours to find where it goes!
    • Stuck? Press Skip to try the next piece. Skipped pieces come back later, and you must place every one of them to finish.
