How to Play & Rules

  • Tap (or click) an intersection to place a stone. You can capture opponent stones by completely surrounding them.
  • You cannot play a suicide move (a point where your own stones would be captured) or a move that violates the ko rule.
  • If you have no good move, choose Pass. If both players pass in a row, the game ends and territory is counted.
  • Scoring uses Chinese rules: stones on the board plus surrounded empty points count as score. White receives 6.5 points of komi.
  • Undo takes the game back to your previous turn.
