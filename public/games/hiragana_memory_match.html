How to Play

Goal: find all the matching pairs hidden on the board.

Playing a turn

  • Click or tap a face-down card to flip it over.
  • Flip a second card. That completes one move.
  • If the two cards are a hiragana and its romaji reading, they stay face up and turn gold.
  • If they do not match, remember where they are — they flip back over after a moment.

Winning

  • The game ends when every pair has been found.
  • Your score is based on moves and time: fewer moves earns more stars.
  • The timer starts on your first flip.

Difficulty

  • Easy — 8 pairs (16 cards). A gentle warm-up.
  • Medium — 12 pairs (24 cards).
  • Hard — 18 pairs (36 cards). A real memory workout.
  • The hiragana used are drawn at random from all 46 basic sounds each game, so no two games are the same.
