How to play

  1. The goal: fill the grid so that every row, every column, and every 3×3 box contains each of the nine kanji — 一 二 三 四 五 六 七 八 九 — exactly once.
  2. Placing a kanji: tap any empty cell to select it, then tap a kanji on the number pad, or press 1–9 on a keyboard. Use the arrow keys to move around the board.
  3. Given clues are printed in bold black and cannot be changed. Your entries appear in blue; a hint-filled cell appears in green.
  4. Mistakes are checked instantly: a wrong kanji turns red and the mistake counter goes up. Fix it with Erase or by entering the right kanji.
  5. Notes mode (pencil icon, or press N) lets you jot down small candidate kanji in an empty cell. Press Backspace to erase, Ctrl+Z to undo, and H for a hint.
  6. Learning the kanji: keep “Small Arabic numbers” on while you learn — a tiny 1–9 appears in the corner of each filled cell. Turn it off for a real challenge once the kanji feel familiar.
  7. Winning: the puzzle is complete when all 81 cells are filled correctly. Every puzzle here has exactly one solution.
  8. Layout: use the Layout switch at the top to choose Side by side (board on the left, number pad and tools on the right) or Board on top (board above the controls). Auto follows the width the game is given — handy when the game is embedded in a page with an iframe: wide frames get the side-by-side layout, narrow frames stack. A page embedding the game can also force a layout with ?layout=side or ?layout=stacked in the frame URL, or by sending a postMessage of {type: "sudoku-layout", layout: "side"}.
